    Questions

    Good to know.

    Still have a question? Call or text (705) 644-3980.

    How long does a tint job take?

    Most vehicles take a few hours, depending on how many windows and the type of vehicle. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

    Why does my new tint look hazy or have small bubbles?

    That's normal while the film cures. Water left between the film and the glass dries out over the next few days, and longer in cold Muskoka weather. Keep your windows rolled up for at least a few days and the haze will clear on its own.

    How do I clean tinted windows?

    Use a soft microfibre cloth and an ammonia-free glass cleaner. Skip scrapers and anything abrasive on the inside of the glass.

    Is dark tint legal in Ontario?

    Ontario has rules about tint on the windshield and front side windows. Braedon will talk through your options before installing, so you get the look you want and know what's allowed.

    Do you tint homes and businesses?

    Yes. Braedon tints residential windows, cottages, storefronts and offices as well as vehicles. Send a few photos or rough measurements through the quote form and he'll get back to you with a price.

    What do the tint percentages mean?

    Tint is measured in VLT (visible light transmission), the percentage of light that gets through the glass. A lower number means a darker window: 70% is very light and 5% is limo. Try different shades on the 3D vehicle in the tint builder.

    What film do you use?

    Braedon installs Scorpion window film in two lines. Scorpion Carbon has a clean matte look, good heat rejection and won't interfere with your phone or GPS. Scorpion Nano IR Ceramic is the premium option, built to block the most infrared heat. Not sure which to pick? Ask and he'll walk you through it.

    How much does it cost?

    Price depends on the vehicle, the number of windows and the film. Send a quick request with your year, make and model, and you'll get a free, no-pressure quote.
